Ph.D. student at IIT Kharagpur · Advised by Prof. Ayan Chowdhury

I work on 3D computer vision, with a focus on understanding the part structure of 3D shapes from point clouds. My current work spans two directions: plant part segmentation and unsupervised 3D shape co-segmentation.

Both build on Point-MAE point cloud features, combined with clustering and prototype-based matching, to produce semantic and instance-level parts that stay consistent across shapes and plant species.

Next, I am extending this to single-image 3D scene understanding: lifting an ordinary photo into a 3D scene and segmenting it into people, objects and their parts, so that part- and co-segmentation work on everyday images rather than only on scanned point clouds.
