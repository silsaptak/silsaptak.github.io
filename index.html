I work on 3D computer vision, with a focus on understanding the part structure of 3D shapes from point clouds using as little supervision as possible. My current work spans two directions: weakly supervised plant part segmentation and unsupervised 3D shape co-segmentation.

Both build on self-supervised point cloud representations (Point-MAE), combined with clustering and prototype-based matching, to produce consistent semantic and instance-level parts across shapes and species with limited annotations.

I am also interested in 3D scene reconstruction and exocentric-to-egocentric (exo→ego) vision: recovering people, scenes and cameras from third-person video and using that geometry to synthesize the first-person view.

Current Work

Ongoing

Weakly Supervised Plant Part Segmentation

Semantic and instance segmentation of plant point clouds (leaves, stems) with limited labels. A shared Point-MAE encoder is trained with reconstruction and contrastive objectives; at inference, plants are over-segmented into fragments, matched against learned part prototypes, and refined with a fragment adjacency graph.

Pheno4D · PLANesT-3D · Point-MAE · Prototypes

Ongoing

Unsupervised 3D Shape Co-segmentation

Discovering consistent parts across a collection of 3D shapes without any part labels. The pipeline uses self-supervised Point-MAE features to group points into parts that correspond across shapes, and is evaluated against prior co-segmentation methods such as AdaCoSeg.

Point Clouds · Self-Supervised Features · Rand Index
